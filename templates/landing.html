Frequently asked questions

Is Do.iT free?

Yes. Create an account and start adding tasks at no cost.

Will the alarm ring when a task is due?

Yes. While Do.iT is open in your browser, it rings at the reminder time with sound, vibration and a notification. Tasks that came due while the app was closed ring as soon as you open it.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Open Do.iT in your phone browser and choose “Add to Home Screen” to use it like an app.
